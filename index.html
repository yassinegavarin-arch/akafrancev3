Who we are

AKA is a Franco Chinese industrial alliance supporting European manufacturers in sourcing, industrialization and quality control. We operate as an extension of our clients purchasing, engineering and quality teams.

What we do

We secure industrial projects from prototype to series production. Our scope covers mechanical welding, machining, plastics, assembly, finishing and supplier quality management.

Our positioning

AKA stands between direct sourcing and traditional trading companies. We combine local Chinese industrial execution with European standards, documentation and accountability.
